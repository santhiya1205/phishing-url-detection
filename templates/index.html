Project Overview

About the System

This web application accepts a website link (URL) as input and predicts whether it is likely to be a legitimate site or a phishing attempt by analysing its structure and content.

Why Phishing Matters

Why Phishing is a Problem

  • Users receive fake links through email, SMS, WhatsApp and social media.
  • Malicious URLs closely resemble real banking, shopping or login websites.
  • Victims may share passwords, OTPs or card details, causing financial loss.
  • Manual verification of every link is time-consuming and error-prone.
